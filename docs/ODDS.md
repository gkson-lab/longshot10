## Difficulties

## Arcade

Arcade rolls are drawn like real rolls but are not recorded. Payback is what a game returns on average for each coin or token played.

### Higher or lower

A roll that a share q of rolls beat is beaten by the next roll with chance exactly q, so calling "rarer" wins with chance q and "commoner" with 1 - q. A right call pays the bet times 0.95 / (its chance), cut to the cent and then rounded down to whole coins: a payback of at most 95%. Bets are 1, 5, 25 or 100 coins.

## Prestige

Prestige k needs 100,000 × k rolls since the previous one (100,000, then 200,000, …). It clears coins, items and active effects, and multiplies every drop and coin chance by 1 + 0.5 × k for good: ×1.5 at prestige 1, ×2 at 2, ×3.5 at 5.

The skin bundle (Gold, Ruby and Aurora) costs 500 coins.
