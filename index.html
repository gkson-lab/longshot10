              Where items come from
              
          
              Each plain roll can drop items, depending on its tier, and drop boosters multiply those chances. Rolls made with a luck charm or the Lens never drop items. Every achievement also gives one, each save file gets a Charm of Ten once a day, and several of one item fuse into the next one up.
